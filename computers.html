Computers & Mini PCs

Compare portable computers and desktop systems by software compatibility, mobility, performance, repairability, accessories, and complete setup cost.

Updated September 13, 2026

Choose the computer family first

💻

Portable Computers

Best when the computer must travel, run on battery, or combine the display, keyboard, camera, speakers, and pointing device in one package.

  • Laptops for general work, school, creative tools, and gaming
  • Tablets and 2-in-1s for touch, pen, reading, and field work
  • Mini PCs and handhelds for compact or specialized setups
Explore Portable Computers →
🖥️

Desktop Systems

Best when the workspace stays put and sustained performance, larger displays, quieter cooling, easier repair, or future upgrades matter more than battery power.

  • Windows desktops for broad software and gaming compatibility
  • Apple desktops for macOS workflows and compact integration
  • Linux and alternative systems for control, learning, servers, and focused jobs
