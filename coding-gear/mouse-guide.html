Mouse Buying Questions

Is a vertical mouse automatically better for wrist comfort?

No single shape fits everyone. Hand size, grip style, desk height, sensitivity, and break habits all matter; try a returnable option when possible.

Do programmable buttons work on every operating system?

Basic pointing usually works broadly, but remapping, gestures, profiles, and firmware updates may require vendor software that is not available on every platform.

Should developers choose Bluetooth or a USB receiver?

Bluetooth saves a port, while a dedicated receiver can offer simpler pairing or lower latency. Multi-device users should verify how switching works and which receiver is included.

The Bottom Line
