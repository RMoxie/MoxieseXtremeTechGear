How to choose the best lock for an e-bike

Protect the frame with a strong primary lock, then add a different second barrier for time, noise, and recovery.

Updated September 8, 2026

E-bikes are attractive theft targets because the frame, battery, and components retain value. A useful security plan combines a strong primary lock, good locking technique, and a second layer that increases time and noise.

Quick answer: use two different lock types

Understand independent security ratings

Independent testing programs such as Sold Secure publish categories and ratings for bicycle locks. Use the current product listing and certificate details rather than relying only on packaging language. For a high-value e-bike, prioritize the stronger bicycle rating your budget and carrying plan allow.

Locking priority: capture the frame and a fixed, immovable anchor with the primary lock. Use the second lock for another part of the frame or a wheel.

Make the lock harder to attack

  • Fill unused space inside a U-lock so tools are harder to position.
  • Keep the lock body and chain off the ground when possible.
  • Choose a busy, well-lit rack that is securely installed.
  • Remove the battery or display when practical and permitted by the manufacturer.
  • Record the frame serial number and keep purchase documentation.

Add recovery and insurance layers

A hidden tracker, registration service, and suitable insurance can improve recovery or reduce loss, but none replaces a strong physical lock. Check tracker alerts, local privacy laws, registration coverage, and the lock requirements in any insurance policy.

Match security to the stop

A café stop in view of the bike is different from an all-day station rack. For longer or predictable parking, add a second substantial lock, vary the location, and consider secure indoor storage.

Frequently asked questions

Is a cable lock enough for an e-bike?

A cable is better used for accessories or as a secondary restraint. A high-value e-bike should be secured to a fixed anchor with a stronger primary lock.

Should I lock through the wheel or frame?

Secure the frame to the fixed anchor first. Use a second lock or cable for a wheel or removable accessory.

Does a GPS tracker prevent theft?

No. A tracker may support alerts or recovery, but it does not replace a strong physical lock and safe parking strategy.
