How affiliate links work

Some links on this site may be affiliate links. If you click an affiliate link and make a qualifying purchase, we may receive a commission from the retailer or affiliate program. The price you pay does not increase because you used our link.

Affiliate relationships help support the ongoing work required to research, write, maintain, and update this site.

Amazon Associates

Moxies eXtreme TechGear participates in the Amazon Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com.

When an Amazon link on this site is an affiliate link, a qualifying purchase may result in a commission to Moxies eXtreme TechGear. We do not receive your payment information or personal purchase details through the affiliate link.

Affiliate links do not buy our recommendations

Affiliate compensation does not determine which products we recommend. Our goal is to help readers make practical, informed technology decisions based on factors such as compatibility, specifications, use case, value, limitations, and available evidence.

We may recommend a product even when an affiliate link is unavailable, and we may discuss products or services from companies with which we have no affiliate relationship.

Prices, availability, and product information

Retail prices, promotions, inventory, specifications, compatibility information, and retailer policies can change. Product links may also change or stop working. Always verify current details at the retailer or manufacturer before purchasing.

Unless a page specifically says otherwise, affiliate links should not be interpreted as proof that we personally tested the product. Our editorial standards distinguish research-based recommendations from hands-on testing.

Editorial independence

We aim to be clear about tradeoffs and limitations rather than presenting every recommendation as universally best. If we discover a meaningful error or outdated claim, we may update the relevant content.

For more information about how we approach research and recommendations, see our About & Editorial Standards page.
