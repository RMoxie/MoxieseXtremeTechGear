Headphone Buying Questions

Are Bluetooth headphones good for competitive gaming?

They can work for casual play, but a dedicated 2.4GHz USB connection or wire usually provides more predictable latency. Confirm platform support before ordering.

Should I buy open-back headphones for an office?

Usually not for a shared office: open backs leak sound and let outside noise in. They make more sense in a quiet private room where a wider soundstage is the priority.

Do studio headphones include a microphone?

Many wired studio models do not. Plan for a separate USB, XLR, or clip-on microphone if calls, streaming, or team chat are important.
