Bloomsburg and beyond

Learn with a recognized dive professional

New divers should begin with a recognized training program and in-person instruction. Experienced divers can use continuing-education courses to build skills for buoyancy, rescue, navigation, enriched air, drysuit, wreck, night, and other environments.
