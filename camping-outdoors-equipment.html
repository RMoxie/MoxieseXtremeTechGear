Outdoor essentials

Build a camping kit that fits your trip

Start with the essentials below, then explore the existing power guides for your charging setup.

⛺

Shelter & campsite setup

Compare tent capacity, packed size, ventilation, rain coverage, and setup complexity. Include the correct stakes, guylines, and a groundsheet sized to the tent.

🛏️

Sleeping bags & pads

Check sleeping-bag comfort ratings and pad insulation together. Compare packed weight and size, and choose a sleep system suited to the conditions you expect.

🎒

Packs & weather protection

Choose a comfortable pack for the complete load. Organize layers, spare socks, and equipment in dry bags or a pack liner; check closures and waterproofing claims.

🍳

Camp kitchen & water storage

Plan cookware, utensils, food storage, and enough carrying capacity for water. Confirm stove-fuel compatibility and site rules before packing.

🔦

Headlamps & campsite lighting

Compare runtime at useful brightness levels, beam patterns, weather resistance, and battery type. Pack a backup light or spare batteries.

🔋

Camping power banks & field charging

Use the existing guide to plan energy needs, compare charging capacity, and build a multi-day charging kit.

Before-you-go equipment checklist

Adapt this list to your destination, trip length, and group; it is not a substitute for local guidance or a complete safety plan.

  • Shelter, stakes, guylines, and a suitable sleep system.
  • Clothing layers, weather protection, and sturdy footwear.
  • Food, cookware, water storage, and an appropriate water plan.
  • Headlamp, backup batteries, charged power bank, and compatible cables.
  • Navigation, communication plan, first-aid supplies, and repair items.
  • Check current weather, access rules, fire restrictions, and campsite requirements with official local sources.
  • Test equipment before departure and carry out your waste.
