How to pick fishing gear that actually gets used

Ten picks, one rule: the best fishing gear is the gear that gets used. A rod that packs down, a bag that holds what you own, a box that keeps hooks dry — here is how to choose.

  • Start with a combo — a rod, reel, and line that already match beats buying parts separately.
  • Match the rod to the water: a 6-foot medium rod covers most freshwater fishing; go telescopic if you travel.
  • A fishfinder pays for itself in fish, not in features — GPS waypoints matter more than screen size.
  • Tackle storage is the difference between fishing and untangling: one good bag and a few 3700 boxes.
  • Gift tip: the lure kit, the pliers, and the Plano box are the safest gifts on this page — no sizing, no setup.
