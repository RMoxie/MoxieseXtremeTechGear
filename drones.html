How the three tiers work

Start with the flying you want to do, not the price tag. Each tier answers a different question:

  • Mini drones: the cheapest way to learn the sticks. Light, portable, and forgiving — ideal first drones for kids and beginners.
  • Mid-range drones: the hobbyist sweet spot. Sharper cameras, longer batteries, and obstacle sensing for confident everyday flying.
  • High-end drones: cinema-grade cameras, extended range, and rock-solid stabilization for creators who sell or showcase their footage.
