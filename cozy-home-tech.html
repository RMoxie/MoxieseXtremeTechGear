Before you buy

How to pick cozy tech that actually gets used

A few checks before you order, so the gift gets used instead of gathering dust.

  • Start with the room you live in most — one smart light or plug there beats five gadgets in a spare room.
  • Check the app and voice support before you buy — Alexa, Google, and Apple Home don't all work with every brand.
  • Winter picks pay back in comfort: a humidifier and a heated blanket cost less than one month of cranking the heat.
