Choose touch-first mobility without losing your workflow.

Pick the design that matches the task

Touch first

Traditional tablets

Excellent for reading, streaming, handwriting, drawing, presentations, and light work. Keyboards and desktop-style apps may be optional or limited.

Detachable

Tablet with keyboard cover

A removable keyboard creates a flexible mobile setup, but lap stability, touchpad size, ports, and accessory cost deserve close attention.

Convertible

360-degree 2-in-1 laptop

A permanent keyboard and desktop operating system support broader workflows, with more weight when used purely as a tablet.

Build the complete tablet budget

  • Keyboard, touchpad, case, and adjustable stand
  • Compatible active pen and replacement tips
  • Enough storage or a realistic cloud-storage plan
  • Charger, cable, hub, and external-display adapter
  • Wi-Fi-only versus cellular hardware and service
  • Accidental-damage coverage and repair options
  • App purchases, subscriptions, and platform migration
  • Backup, account recovery, and device tracking
Test the hardest task before replacing a laptop.

Open the largest document, run the real video call, connect the required display or printer, move files between services, and use the exact keyboard and pen. A short hands-on test reveals more than a feature list.

Display, pen, and portability checks

Screen size improves split-screen work but increases weight. Brightness and reflection matter outdoors. Artists and note-takers should confirm pen compatibility, latency, pressure features, palm rejection, and whether the pen charges and stores securely.

For travel, compare the weight of the tablet with keyboard, case, pen, and charger. Check port placement, biometric unlock, camera position during landscape calls, speaker quality, and whether the device can charge while connected to a hub.

Tablet buying questions

Can a tablet replace a laptop?

A tablet can replace a laptop when its apps, file handling, browser, external display support, keyboard, and peripherals cover the complete workflow. Test essential tasks rather than assuming a desktop app has an equal tablet version.

Should I buy a tablet or a Windows 2-in-1?

Choose a tablet for touch-first use and a 2-in-1 when full desktop applications and laptop-style file handling matter. Keyboard comfort, weight, ports, and battery behavior can differ greatly.

Do I need cellular service on a tablet?

Cellular can help frequent travelers and field workers, but it adds device and service cost. Compare hotspot use, coverage, plan terms, eSIM support, and security needs.
