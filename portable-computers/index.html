Plan the complete setup—not just the computer.

Budget for the charger, dock, keyboard, pen, case, monitor, storage, warranty, and adapters you will actually need. A cheaper device can cost more after missing essentials are added.

A practical shortlist process

  1. List the programs, websites, peripherals, and accounts that must work.
  2. Decide whether the device will live at a desk, travel daily, or do both.
  3. Set minimum needs for memory, storage, battery, display, ports, and connectivity.
  4. Compare the complete price with essential accessories and support.
  5. Confirm return terms, warranty service, and replacement parts before buying.
