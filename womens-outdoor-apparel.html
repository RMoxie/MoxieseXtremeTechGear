Before you buy

How to pick outdoor apparel that fits

Fit is the difference between gear you love and gear that sits in the closet — a few checks before you order.

  • Men’s and women’s fits are cut differently — check the brand’s size chart, not just your usual size.
  • Apparel prices on Amazon vary by size and color — the price shown is for the variant named on the card.
  • Layer it: base layer wicks sweat, mid-layer holds warmth, shell blocks wind and rain.
  • Check the return policy before ordering — free returns make it easy to swap a size.
  • Try everything on at home before the trip: walk, squat, and reach overhead.
