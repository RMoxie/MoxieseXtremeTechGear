Buying checklist

Three checks before you buy

1. Vehicle compatibility

Confirm voltage, engine-size limits, tire size, connector type, and operating temperature.

2. Power readiness

Choose USB-C charging when practical, record recharge intervals, and inspect stored batteries every few months.

3. Safe operation

Prioritize reverse-polarity protection, clear pressure presets, bright lighting, and controls usable with gloves.

Build in stages

A kit for every budget

Essential

Start with the tire inflator, reflective triangle, flashlight, first aid, and your roadside-assistance details.
