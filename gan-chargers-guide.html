How to Choose a Fast Wall Charger

  • Start with laptop wattage: Match the charger's single-port output to the highest-power device you plan to carry.
  • Read the port-allocation chart: A 160W or 200W total is divided when several ports are active. Confirm the exact combination you expect to use.
  • Check charging standards: USB Power Delivery handles most modern USB-C devices; PPS improves compatibility with supported phones, and PD 3.1 EPR supports higher-power laptops.
  • Use a rated cable: The charger, device, and USB-C cable all have to support the target wattage. For the highest-power charging, use the appropriately marked high-wattage cable.
  • Choose the right form: Wall-mounted bricks are easier to pack; vertical desktop chargers provide more ports and help organize a permanent setup.

Buying tip: More total wattage does not automatically mean faster charging for every device. The useful number is the output available to the specific port combination you will use.
