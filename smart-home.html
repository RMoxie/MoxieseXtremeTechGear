Smart Home

Prices checked October 24, 2026

A house that runs itself, one room at a time. Start with a speaker and a few plugs, then add climate, lighting, and entry as the budget allows — every pick here works with the others. If your angle is safety rather than automation, see our Home Safety & Security page for alarms, cameras, and doorbells.

Smart home gear fails when it's bought as a pile of gadgets. It wins when it's bought as a system: one voice assistant, one lighting family, one app for the door. This guide is organized the way you'd actually build it — voice first, then comfort, then light, then entry — and every link is one we verified ourselves.
