Full-Auto BB Guns

Prices checked October 28, 2026

Full-auto BB guns are the most fun you can have in the backyard without a range membership: .177 steel BBs, select-fire fun, and prices that make them genuinely giftable. These ten came from friends asking for Christmas picks, so every one is a rifle (plus one compact pistol) that is fun out of the box. Every link below was verified against its exact Amazon listing before it earned a card.

Safety first, always. Always wear eye protection, never point any airgun at people or animals, and treat every BB gun like a real firearm for handling and storage. Check your state and local laws before ordering — some sellers restrict shipping by state and require an adult signature, and CO2 cartridges and BBs are usually sold separately.
