Match storage to the workload

Everyday upgrades benefit most from dependable capacity and warranty coverage. Large media projects, games, and local AI models may justify sustained write performance, DRAM, or a higher-end controller. Laptop and compact builds also require attention to heat, physical clearance, and power use.
