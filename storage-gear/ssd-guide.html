SSD Buying Questions

Will a PCIe 4.0 SSD work in a PCIe 3.0 slot?

Many NVMe drives are backward compatible, but they will run at the slower interface speed. Confirm the motherboard or laptop manual, M.2 keying, and physical length.

Does every high-performance SSD need a heatsink?

Cooling needs depend on the drive, workload, airflow, and existing motherboard cover. Never stack an aftermarket heatsink on top of a built-in one.

Why is my portable SSD slower than its advertised speed?

The computer, port, cable, enclosure protocol, file size, and thermal conditions can all limit throughput. A 20Gbps drive needs a compatible 20Gbps host to approach its headline rate.
